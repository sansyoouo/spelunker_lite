# spelunker lite
A simplified version of the Spelunker mod co-developed for mosyusi's Minecraft modpack.
## Added cause of death
### Sprained ankle
If you run, you will sprain your ankle and die in about 10 seconds (to be precise, it performs a 1 in 200 probability calculation every tick).
### spasm
Without any warning, seizures occur at intervals of about 10 minutes (more precisely, a calculation of 1/12,000 is performed every tick), and death results.
### drown
If you touch water, you will drown. The same is true if it rains.
### blood shortage
If your physical strength drops below 20, you will die from shock due to lack of blood.
### craft failure
There's a 1 in 10 chance that crafting will fail and result in a massive explosion.
### drug addiction
If you have a buff-type effect active, you will die from drug poisoning.

Death in this mod basically deals 40 damage, which can be avoided depending on the combination of mods. However, crafting failure deals 1024 damage, which is almost impossible to prevent.
